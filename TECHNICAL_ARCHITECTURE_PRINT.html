Status: Production Architecture

Release Date: October 2026

1. Architectural Thesis & Value Proposition

The platform bridges physical factory telemetry with semantic operational reasoning. Traditional analytics rely strictly on tabular SQL aggregations, missing complex root-cause linkages and equipment containment. By pairing DuckDB (vectorized local OLAP) with TypeDB 2.29 (Polymorphic Entity-Relation Knowledge Graph) under a sub-millisecond JEV Fast-Router and LangGraph coordinator, the platform delivers instantaneous answers to both quantitative ("How much downtime?") and causal ("What was the cascading disruption of this production order?") questions.
